When influent flow and composition change, how does dissolved oxygen feedback affect effluent quality and energy use?

**SIMPO 1.8.0** adds support for **Benchmark Simulation Model No. 1 (BSM1)**. BSM1 defines a reference activated sludge plant, influent scenarios, and performance indicators. In SimpoDash, you can compare open-loop and closed-loop operation under the same influent conditions.

![BSM1 closed-loop flowsheet in SimpoDash, showing five reactors, a secondary clarifier, recycle lines, and two PI control loops.](/img/bsm1/flowsheet.png)

## Build and compare BSM1 scenarios

- **Takacs Settling Tank** supports 3 to 10 layers. BSM1 uses 10 layers to model settling in its secondary clarifier.
- **KLa Pump** takes the volumetric oxygen transfer coefficient directly, in d⁻¹. [See the BSM1 aeration setup](/docs/CaseStudy/BSM1/Setup#aeration).
- **Controller** connects a sensor, a PI controller, and a control handle. In BSM1, nitrate in Tank2 controls the internal recycle, and dissolved oxygen in Tank5 controls aeration. [See both control loops](/docs/CaseStudy/BSM1/Setup#controller).
- **Assessment** calculates BSM1 indicators such as effluent quality (EQ) and the overall cost index (OCI), and supports custom metrics. [Compare the dry-weather assessment reports](/docs/CaseStudy/BSM1/Assessment#first-assessment).

The [BSM1 case study](/docs/CaseStudy/BSM1#projects) includes eight public projects covering open-loop and closed-loop operation with constant, dry-weather, rain, and storm influent. You can inspect saved results or sign in and clone a project to rerun it.

## How closely does SIMPO reproduce the MATLAB results? {#closed-loop-comparison}

We reran **case 04, dry weather with feedback**, lowering SIMPO’s **Step Tolerance from 5% to 0.01%**. The oxygen controller plots above use the new case 04 run at 5%. The table compares the tighter run with MATLAB R2024b under the same plant and input conditions. Controller metrics cover the full **14 days**, with one sample every 15 minutes. Plant Assessment covers **days 7–14**.

<Case04Comparison />

At 0.01% Step Tolerance, the largest relative controller difference is **0.02281%**, for the mean absolute process error in the oxygen loop. Both assessments now use the day 13, 23:45 inventory through `Change(inventory, -1)`. After aligning the input times and closing sample, the SP difference changed from **−0.03284** to **+0.00129 kg SS/d**. Wasting still covers all seven days. [Check the method and WNSE for all eight cases](/docs/CaseStudy/BSM1/Results#wnse).
