### Performance Engine Refactoring

This update represents a deep, core-level optimization.
We have not only upgraded the underlying engine to the first stable version of **Python 3.13**,
but also brought new improvements in multi-threading concurrency, algorithm expansion, modeling flexibility, and process simulation accuracy.

---

### Deep Optimization of Multi-threaded Performance

In **version 1.3.0**, we have refactored and optimized the underlying thread management, further maximizing your computer's multi-core performance:

- **Simulation**: Optimized equation solving, resulting in faster computation speeds.
- **Sensitivity/Uncertainty/Estimation**: Parallel computation threads have been significantly optimized, eliminating long wait times for massive parameter evaluations.
- **Dynamic Plotting**: Dynamic plots during computation will have a reduced impact on the main computation thread's speed. If **Real-Time Plotting** is disabled during computation, the computation speed will be further improved.

In the case study from the paper [SIMPO—A Simplified Intelligent Modelling Platform Online for Code-free and Shareable Wastewater Treatment Process Modelling](https://www.sciengine.com/WE/doi/10.1016/j.wateco.2025.100021):
- Computation speed for 1 million **Uncertainty** runs decreased from **4658s** to **25s**, a performance improvement of **99%**.
- Computation speed for large-scale **Estimation** runs decreased from **267s** to **5s**, a performance improvement of **98%**.

For specific calculation results, please see: [AD with sulphide V21](https://dash.simpowater.com/dashboard/projects/public/name/AD%20with%20sulphide/id/1484/did/415).

---

### RFGA Algorithm

To provide more powerful parameter estimation and optimization capabilities, we have introduced the **RFGA** algorithm into the **Estimation** computation engine.
This new algorithm will offer you more dimensional solution choices and higher convergence efficiency when solving complex model parameters.

---

### Other Optimizations

- **SBR Simulation**: Corrected the calculation formula for variable volume (when `Constant Volume` is `False`). The **CAS** process can now accurately approximate the simulation of the **SBR** process.
- **Wider Parameter Range**: The parameter ranges for `BioModel.Parameter` and `Solution.Variable` now support negative values, meeting the needs of more scenarios.
- **Solution Caching Support**: Saves computation initialization time.
