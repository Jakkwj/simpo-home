After completing a simulation, you may want to look beyond concentration curves: does the system's mass balance close? In which tanks is a component mainly produced, and which reactions consume it?

**SIMPO 1.6.0** introduces **Balance Analysis**, using Sankey diagrams to display mass balances and reaction contributions, making simulation results easier to check and interpret. SimpoClient also supports updates directly from the command line, alongside improvements to account sign-in and the Dashboard.

## Balance Analysis: From Concentration Curves to Mass Balances

You can click the **Balance Analysis** icon on the simulation results page to view mass balance and reaction analysis results for the current calculation.

### Mass Balance: Check the System's Complete Balance

Where does material enter the system? Which processes produce or consume it, and where does it leave? Mass Balance brings these contributions together in a single Sankey diagram to help you check the balance within the system boundary.

- **External inputs and outputs**: examine contributions crossing the system boundary, including influent, effluent, and sludge wasting.
- **Reactions and physical processes**: distinguish biochemical reactions, physical mass transfer, Constraint compensation, and other balance terms.
- **Changes in stored mass**: dynamic calculations also account for the change between the initial and final mass stored in the tanks.

Select an accounting item under **Composition, Component, or Target**, such as an elemental composition defined in the model, an individual component, or a combined indicator defined through Conversion. The width of each flow in the Sankey diagram represents its contribution. Use the balance summary and detailed tables below the diagram to check the values further.

### Reaction Analysis: Identify Sources of Production and Consumption

A component can be produced and consumed at the same time. Looking only at its final concentration or net change can make it difficult to understand the role of each reaction.

**Reaction Analysis** shows total production, total consumption, and net production separately, preserving the contributions of individual tanks and reactions. Expand tank nodes to inspect reaction details and compare the main sources of production or consumption.

For example, when analysing ammonium nitrogen, you can examine its production and consumption across tanks and reactions, then use the influent, effluent, and stored-mass changes in Mass Balance to help interpret the simulation results.

## Update SimpoClient from the Command Line

Keeping your calculation client up to date is now easier! SimpoClient lets you view the current version, check for a new version, and update directly from your terminal.

View the current version:

```bash
SimpoClient --version
```

Check whether a new version is available:

```bash
SimpoClient update --check
```

Update to the latest version:

```bash
SimpoClient update
```

## Account and Interface Improvements

- **GitHub and Google registration and sign-in**: you can now register or sign in to SIMPO using a third-party account.
- **Account deletion**: account management now includes an option to delete your account.
- **Column dropdown filters**: Dashboard tables now let you filter data through column dropdown menus, making it easier to find what you need.
- **Activity calendar chart**: a new Activity calendar chart on the Dashboard home page helps you view activity records.
- **Layouts for different screen sizes**: page layouts and display have been improved across different screen sizes.
